Questions

Good to know

Still unsure? You can call us on +44 7538 659384.

Does it cost anything?

No. Entry, dinner and the draw are free, and you will not be asked to pay at any point.

What exactly is the scholarship?

It pays the full tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at a Universal Study Advisors partner university in the UK. Living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge and travel are not included, and the winner must meet the university's normal entry requirements.

Do I need to be there to win?

Yes. Only guests checked in with original photo ID on the night go into that evening's draw.

When does booking close?

At 23:59 the night before each event: Wednesday 28 October for London, Sunday 1 November for Birmingham and Wednesday 4 November for Manchester. Seats are limited, so a city may close earlier if it fills up.

Which ID should I bring?

An original passport or UK photo driving licence. Photocopies and phone photos are not accepted, and the name must match your booking.

When will my guest pass arrive?

After our team has reviewed your booking, by email or SMS before the event. Check your spam folder if nothing has arrived.

Can a friend come with me?

Each pass admits one student. Your friend can book their own seat on this page.

Terms

Scholarship draw terms

The key points: free to enter; one 100% tuition fee scholarship per event; register online by 23:59 the day before your event; attend in person with original photo ID; winner drawn at random on the night.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Promoter. Universal Study Advisors.
  2. Free entry. The draw is free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or to win.
  3. Who can enter. UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status. Employees of Universal Study Advisors, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. (a) Send a seat request through this page by 23:59 (UK time) on the day before your chosen event; (b) receive an approved ticket from Universal Study Advisors; (c) attend the event in person and check in with an original passport or UK photo driving licence matching the name on your request. One entry per person, for one event only.
  5. Events and closing dates. London, Thursday 29 October 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 28 October). Birmingham, Monday 2 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 1 November). Manchester, Thursday 5 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 4 November). Doors open at 17:00 and dinner starts at 18:00.
  6. Seats. Seats at each venue are limited and are given at the Promoter's discretion after reviewing each request. Not every request will receive a ticket.
  7. The prize. One prize per event (three in total): a scholarship covering 100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one postgraduate taught (Master's) course at a UK partner university of Universal Study Advisors. The scholarship is valid only for one-year (full-time) Master's courses at BPP University and cannot be applied to any other university or course length.
  8. What the prize does not cover. Living costs, accommodation, visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, travel, deposits or any other cost. No cash alternative is offered. The prize cannot be transferred.
  9. Conditions of the prize. The winner must meet the university's academic and English language entry requirements, receive and accept an offer, and meet any UK immigration requirements for the course.
  10. The draw. One winner per event is drawn at random, live at the event, from guests checked in with valid ID. The winner must be present when drawn; if not, another winner is drawn.
  11. Winner announcement. The winner is announced on the night. With the winner's consent, their first name and city may be published on Universal Study Advisors's website and social media.

  14. Changes. The Promoter may change or cancel an event or the draw if circumstances outside its control require it, and will give notice where possible.
